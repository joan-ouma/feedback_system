Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is my identity truly anonymous?

Yes, completely. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number. You sign up with a cryptographically generated token. No one, including us, can trace any activity back to you.

What happens if I lose my token?

Your token is the only way to access your account. If you lose it, you will need to create a new account. We strongly recommend saving it somewhere safe immediately after signup.

Is the AI counselor a real person?

No, the counselor is an AI trained specifically for student mental health support. It is designed to listen, offer coping strategies, and suggest campus resources. For urgent crises, please contact emergency services immediately.

Who can see my consultations and data?

Your AI consultation sessions and mood data are completely private and are never shared with anyone.

Is the platform available 24/7?

Yes. The platform and the AI counselor are available at any time of day or night, every day of the week. Reach out whenever you need support, no appointment required.
