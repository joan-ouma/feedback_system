If You Need A Moment

🫁

Box Breathing

Inhale for 4 counts. Hold for 4. Exhale for 4. Hold for 4. Repeat 4 times. A simple reset for your nervous system.

🌿

Ground Yourself

Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 you taste. The 5-4-3-2-1 technique.
